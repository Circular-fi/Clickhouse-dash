# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be respectful, pragmatic, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical discussion and project goals.
- Assume good intent, but be open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or intimidation
- Discrimination or hateful conduct
- Bad-faith disruption, trolling, or personal attacks
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that harms the project or its contributors. Repeated or severe violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from participation.

## Reporting

Report problematic behavior privately to the maintainers when possible.
