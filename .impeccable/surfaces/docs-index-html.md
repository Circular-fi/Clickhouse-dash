# Surface brief: ClickHouse Dash home page (docs/index.html)

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: ClickHouse operators (data, SRE, platform). Action: download v1.0.0 and run it. Proof: the 74 s video, real screenshots, the OpenTelemetry contrib exporter compatibility, 6.6 MB static binary. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, no build step, English only, Circular.fi logo placed prominently. Direction pinned by the owner: very dynamic, real-time 3D and motion design, Circular's infra and data world. Roll skipped (brief-pinned).

## Direction contract

THESIS: ClickHouse is columnar and Dash is built of columns, so the page is a live 3D field of columns (WebGL) that rises and falls like a stream of data, and every motion on the page is that stream resolving into the product. It refuses the centred-headline-over-three-cards SaaS page and the fake dashboard screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Circular's deep water: ground #07111f, panels #0b182a, lines #1a3150, text #f4fbff, muted #93a9bb, one accent aqua #45e7e1 (the only UI accent; the Circular logo keeps its own blues). Geist display and body, Geist Mono for commands and numbers. Soft 20 px containers, pill buttons. Light comes from the top of the columns; fog fades them into the ground. Recognisable with the text removed: a navy field of aqua-lit columns.

STORY: A visitor sees a living column field and the product name, understands within seconds it is a ClickHouse dashboard in one binary, watches the real video un-tilt and play in a 3D frame, learns in five panels what it covers (Query, Library, Explorer, Observability on the OpenTelemetry contrib exporter tables, System, MCP), then picks a platform and copies three commands. Circular closes the page as the publisher.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport WebGL column field behind everything, camera tilted down, columns concentrated right and bottom, a vignette keeping the left dark. Left, bottom-aligned: a brand strip (Dash mark and the Circular logo with "Built by Circular.fi"), a two-line headline at about 72 px, one 20-word sentence, primary "Download v1.0.0" and secondary "Watch the tour". The nav is one 64 px line. The video frame peeks in at the bottom edge, tilted in perspective.

FORM: 3D data-landscape hero plus a scroll-choreographed product story (video theatre with chapter rail, sticky screenshot stack, data-flow strip, install panel). Own composition, not from a dealt list; signature interaction: the column field reacts to the pointer and to the scroll, and the video frame un-tilts as it arrives. Seed key: none (owner-pinned). Motion grammar: transform and opacity only, spring-like easing, everything stills under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
