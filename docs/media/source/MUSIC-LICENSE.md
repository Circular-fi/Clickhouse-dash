# Music: source and licence

**Track.** "Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 12" by Sascha Ende (ende.app), 1:58, instrumental (clean electric guitar, a driving
beat and a groovy bass, about 110 BPM). It is the file `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` that the `brag`
skill bundles in `assets/music/`. The video uses it from 1.6 s into the track (a whole number of beats, so the cuts are on the
beat and the closing card lands on a strong beat), cut at the end of the video with a 3 s fade out.

**What was checked** (the track page and the licence page of the author, on 2026-10-12):

* The track page <https://ende.app/en/song/12881-happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12> gives the duration 1:58 (the same as the bundled
  file), the licence **CC BY 4.0** and says: "free for private and commercial projects"; "the one condition is attribution".
  Its attribution text: "Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 12 by Sascha Ende - ende.app License: CC BY 4.0 (ende.app/standard-license)".
* The licence page <https://ende.app/en/standard-license> says that the licence is CC BY 4.0 "with an upgrade": for this author's
  music **no credit is required** (a credit is welcome, "Music by Sascha Ende at ende.app"). Commercial use is allowed. Putting
  the music in videos, podcasts or games is allowed. Modifying it is allowed ("cut it, loop it, add effects"). The music must be
  part of your own work (background, intro, ...).
* What it forbids: uploading the music unchanged as your own song to music services; registering it with YouTube Content ID or a
  similar system; and it gives no warranty ("at your own risk").

**What is not said, so what is not verified.**

* The pages do not speak of redistributing the audio **file** itself in a public repository. This repository does not contain it:
  `composition/assets/music/` is generated (see `README.md`) and is not committed. Only the finished video, which uses the track
  as a background under the narration, is committed, which is the use that the licence describes (music as part of a work).
  CC BY 4.0 itself allows sharing and adapting with attribution, so a public repository is, in our reading, within the licence, but
  this is our reading and not a statement of the author.
* Other volumes of the series are described by the site as made "with licensed AI-assisted tools" (Vol. 11 and 14 pages) while the
  artist's profile says "no AI assistance". The page of Vol. 12 does not say either way. If a "no AI-generated music" rule applies
  to this repository, ask the author or use the voice-only variant.
* Pages can change: the terms above are those read on 2026-10-12. Nothing here is legal advice.

**Credit.** The closing card of the video carries "Music: Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 12 by Sascha Ende, ende.app, CC BY 4.0".
Keep it, or keep the credit line wherever the video is posted.

**Do not** register the video with Content ID (the licence forbids it for the music).

**Sound effects and voice.** The sound
effects (cursor clicks, key presses, a soft hit on each cut) are CC0 (Kenney.nl, and "Keyboard Soundpack #1" by unicae_games on
OpenGameArt, CC0), bundled with the `brag` skill. The voice is generated locally with Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0 model) through Hyperframes.
